# Bounded literature note: real degeneracy cases and evaluation

Checked 2026-10-22. This is a focused implementation note, not a systematic novelty survey.

|---|---|---|---|
| [SuperLoc, ICRA 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02901) | Predicts alignment risk before registration from directional observability contributions of raw scan/map correspondences; selectively fuses alternative odometry in weak directions. | Uses cave, multi-floor, tunnel/long-corridor field runs, with a FARO-derived reference map; reports map inlier/outlier proportions at a 10 cm threshold and odometry robustness/outlier results. | Record which pose direction is weak and compare prediction against map localization under the same sequence; raw scan anisotropy is only a screening proxy, not the paper’s correspondence-based risk measure. |

## What this means for the current hypothesis

Eigen/singular-value degeneracy checks, directional observability, motion-prior use, and constrained updates are established ideas. This round is not testing a new mitigation algorithm; it asks whether the fixed R14B PCL NDT pipeline exhibits a real failure under one input-only-selected window. The prescreen covariance ratio is deliberately weaker than the correspondence- or optimization-conditioned measures used by these papers. It cannot establish a formal NDT weak direction or be reported as a degeneracy result.

The directly transferable experimental practice is: (1) freeze a single difficult sequence/window without looking at estimator error; (2) retain a baseline under a shared initial condition; (3) report direction-resolved information and per-frame reference-relative position/orientation errors, plus failure/outlier or drift behavior; and (4) keep the benchmark reference provenance explicit. Newer College `01_short_experiment` provides a map-anchored benchmark reference, not independent motion capture, so R16 errors remain reference-relative.
