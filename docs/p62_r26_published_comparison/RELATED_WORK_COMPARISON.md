## Two closest comparisons to carry into the paper

**2Fast-2Lamaa:** closest in prior-map localization, resource awareness and Newer College reporting. Its new continuous representation/undistortion is a method contribution unlike our frozen-solver pipeline. The smallest honest claim is that the placement of one geometry veto can make a bounded retry mechanism unreachable in an existing NDT/filter implementation, and a controlled causal ablation distinguishes removing that veto from obtaining an admissible alternate initialization. This is a measured system-design distinction, not evidence of superior localization or an invention of event-based search. Exact paper initialization is NR at the required controlled-window scope.

**SuperLoc:** closest in the responsibility assigned to pre-registration information. Both consider information before optimization; SuperLoc predicts directional alignment risk and modifies supporting fusion, whereas G stops an inherited distance/overlap predicate from prohibiting all optimization. Our result concerns that specific predicate at a perturbed causal prediction. It does not imply that geometry should never influence registration, or that SuperLoc's pre-optimization analysis would exhibit the same failure.

Koide2024 provides a third useful integration contrast: robust prior-map localization through tightly coupled factors rather than a new gate/restart around a frozen solver. Its official project identifies the implementation as closed-source; current GLIM software is not assumed to be the identical published baseline.

## Originality assessment

No verified source in this bounded review establishes an identical implementation-level C+G ablation, but absence in seven papers is not proof of originality. The work can present a reproducible engineering contribution and causal failure analysis. A journal-level methodological novelty or SOTA performance claim is **NOT_ESTABLISHED**. In particular, simply combining familiar ingredients and obtaining one successful short-window recovery cannot justify a broad new degeneracy-aware algorithm claim.

The retained study has real value in separating optimizer access, optimizer convergence, post-quality admission, actual filter update and later reference-relative error. The G-only negative ablation is central:60 converged nominal registrations,0admissions; C+G has57map updates with8extra calls. Rejected D/E/F/Coupled explorations remain boundaries, not contributions folded into C+G.
