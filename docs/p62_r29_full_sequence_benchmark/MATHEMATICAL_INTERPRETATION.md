# Mechanism interpretation and boundaries (no new optimization formula)

1. Pre-alignment nearest-map distances are evaluated at the prediction. A failed predicate can represent initial misalignment rather than absence of recoverable geometric support. G removes its veto authority, preserving the same diagnostic values and post-optimization safeguards.
2. Native convergence does not imply acceptable observation quality. G-only can fail because the optimized pose fails basic/consistency rules, because the consistency input is unavailable, or because the real predictor/map support is invalid. These causes must be separated by logs rather than attributed to a local minimum automatically.
3. Fixed yaw starts sample different trajectories of the unchanged nonlinear Gaussian optimizer. Different terminal outputs are possible, but a finite restart budget neither proves global optimization nor guarantees a useful alternative.
4. Coverage Consistency Mean uses points also optimized by Full NDT. It is not independent holdout evidence or ground truth. Valid-mask/Overlap/.30 admission does not certify correct pose; unavailable consistency input is distinct from a large finite loss.
5. A fixed frame-count budget bounds calls, not worst-case CPU time. Restart cost depends on source/target geometry and iterations. An accepted recovery must be distinguished from a candidate selection, a published measurement, an actual OOSM/EKF update and a sustained reference-relative improvement.
6. Snapshot-depth adaptation preserves header times and measurements. Missing IMU intervals stay missing under the frozen model. Stale causal predictions are rejected, never replaced with future state or a test reference. A failure associated with raw sampling gaps must not be automatically called NDT basin failure.
7. P45 map selection follows each policy's actual prediction. Equal map assets/settings can produce different selected targets after histories diverge. Target identities, counts and switches are therefore part of the controlled system results, not hidden constants.
8. Newer College H_B/H_C are fixed alternative coordinate assumptions, not candidates selected by accuracy. Koide references are batch map-anchored estimates. Neither is independent motion capture. Any eventual benefit is restricted to the actual sensor/protocol and cannot establish a new NDT objective, new IMU model or universal threshold.

## Observed complete-sequence evidence

The actual result supports none of the desired multistart recovery claims: 132 additional native converged outputs, 131 physically different from their nominal match, zero basic-eligible alternatives and zero selected alternatives. B/C poses are identical. A finite Consistency Mean below .30 can nevertheless accompany large benchmark-relative pose error (raw131: .240642 with 5.532m/13.234deg). Thus neither optimizer convergence, different termination nor quality admission establishes correct recovery. The frozen system also propagates long stretches without map updates while raw IMU gaps remain unintegrated; these observations cannot isolate a single root cause. No new formula is proposed to explain away the failure.
