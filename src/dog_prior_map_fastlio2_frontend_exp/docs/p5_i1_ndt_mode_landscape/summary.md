## Formal NDT audit

- Implementation source: `src/dog_prior_map_localization/src/external_ndt_transaction_server.cpp`.
- Class/library: `pcl::NormalDistributionsTransform<pcl::PointXYZ,pcl::PointXYZ>`, PCL registration package `1.10.0+dfsg-5ubuntu1`.
- Settings: resolution 0.8 m; step size 0.08; transformation epsilon 0.001; maximum iterations 40. Search uses PCL NDT target-cell radius search at the resolution.
- Source filter: finite points, range 0.5–80 m, 0.25 m voxel grid, cap 1,400. Target is the full fixed map, finite filtered and voxelized twice at 0.15 m. No local map crop.
- Anchor: saved request `predicted_map_T_lidar`; perturbations are right/body `T_seed = T0 Exp(delta)`. Final transform convention is `map_T_lidar`.
- `getFitnessScore()` is PCL's nearest-neighbor mean squared-distance diagnostic (lower is better); it is not the NDT optimizer objective. `getTransformationProbability()` is the normalized PCL NDT score and is not an absolute posterior probability.
- Objective access: the standalone helper exposes PCL `computeDerivatives` at a fixed transform. Its summed score is the score maximized by the PCL alignment code (higher is better). The Hessian used below is PCL's optimizer-reported analytic score Hessian at each representative pose, with the same target-cell radius-search neighborhood logic. It is not Fisher information or a statistical covariance.

## Seed domain and clustering

Each frame received 245 planar seeds and 18 single-axis probes. The eight targeted frames received 48 additional wide planar-ring seeds with yaw `{-15, 0, +15} deg`; total runs were 8,800. Every seed was generated by right/body perturbation `T0 Exp(delta)`. No GT-informed seed or search-range adjustment was used.

Clustering is deterministic complete-link. Primary cutoffs are translation `<=0.20 m` and rotation `<=2 deg`; strict sensitivity is `0.10 m / 1 deg`, loose sensitivity `0.40 m / 4 deg`. A cluster is tagged `STABLE_MODE_CANDIDATE` only if it has at least 5 converged seeds and at least 2% of that frame's converged seed domain. Cluster representatives are actual converged poses selected by objective score. `K=0` means no cluster passed this diagnostic tag, not “no local extremum.”

| Strict | 15/32 (46.9%) | 3/19 (15.8%) | 4/8 (50.0%) |

At the primary cutoff, all-frame counts are `K=0:9, K=1:6, K=2:2, K>=3:15`; the maximum K is 8. For the 19 selected frames at or after 150 s, counts are `K=0:9, K=1:4, K=2:2, K=4:1, K=5:2, K=6:1`. The targeted cohort counts are `K=0:2, K=1:2, K=3:4`.

Mean empirical stable-basin entropy is 0.973 nats over the 23 frames with at least one tagged stable mode (single-mode rows contribute zero); it is 0.757 over 10 such >=150 s frames and 0.523 over 6 targeted frames. These are fractions over the fixed converged seed domains, not posterior probabilities. Targeted frames have 48 additional wide seeds, so their sampling domains differ from control-only frames.

There are 192 primary stable-mode pairs. Pairwise separation median is 0.871 m / 3.341 deg; maximum is 3.857 m / 22.475 deg. The complete pair table and basin-weighted inter-mode scatter are saved; neither is a posterior covariance.

Multimode structure is not specific to the failure interval: uniform controls are 3/3 multimode in 0–50 s and 3/3 in 50–100 s, while 150–200 s uniform controls are 0/3. The separately sampled targeted failure cohort is 4/8 multimode. The observation depends on the finite seed domain and clustering thresholds.

## GT post-hoc comparison

Only after the GT-blind outputs were frozen, the official IMU-origin GT convention and fixed first-common anchor used by P3-R10C were applied. Nine frames had `K=0`, so no stable candidate was available for nearest-mode comparison. Among the remaining 23 frames, the baseline-associated cluster was also GT-nearest in 8 and differed in 15.

Across those 23 comparable frames, mean baseline and GT-nearest stable-candidate translation deviations were 5.077 m and 4.953 m. On the 15 identity mismatches, the means were 3.488 m and 3.322 m; mean objective ranks were 22.33 for the baseline cluster and 6.60 for the GT-nearest cluster. Some mismatches have the baseline pose marginally closer to GT; identity mismatch alone is not evidence that the baseline is worse. GT was used only for this post-hoc comparison.

## Local curvature and pure-degeneracy check

For each of 108 GT-blind representatives (stable candidates plus any baseline-associated nonstable cluster), the helper evaluated PCL's analytic `computeDerivatives` score Hessian at the representative transform. PCL parameter coordinates are `[tx,ty,tz,roll,pitch,yaw]`; the score is the sum used by `align`, evaluated at fixed transform with the current radius-search target-cell neighborhoods. The raw Hessian and resolution-normalized Hessian are saved. For `u=[tx/r,ty/r,tz/r,roll,pitch,yaw]`, scaling is `H_u = D^T H_xi D`, `D=diag(r,r,r,1,1,1)`, `r=0.8 m`. Since PCL maximizes the score, local-maximum curvature is inspected using `-H_score`.

All 108 analytic Hessians were finite and negative-definite after sign reversal; maximum raw asymmetry before symmetrization was `9.2e-7`. The analytic extraction gate therefore passed. A separate full 6x6 central finite-difference sensitivity audit used `h_t=max(0.01,0.02r)=0.016 m`, `h_r=0.25 deg`, and half/base/double steps. It is a cross-check, not the primary Hessian. Under the spectrum/order test, 96/108 (88.9%) finite-difference estimates were step-sensitive; relative Frobenius difference between base-step FD and PCL analytic Hessian had median 1.152, P95 8.336, maximum 11.007. This mismatch limits strong interpretation of a local curvature magnitude and is preserved in the CSV rather than hidden.

For uniform controls, median baseline-associated minimum eigenvalue of `-H_scaled` is 2763.17 (n=3) in 0–50 s and 1383.93 (n=3) in 150–200 s; corresponding median condition numbers are 254.11 and 401.89. Only one 150–200 s uniform frame has `K=1`; its minimum eigenvalue is 1383.93 and condition 401.89. This small, selected comparison is not enough to designate geometric degeneracy. No pure single-mode degeneracy is confirmed; absence is not proven. Local curvature extraction passes, while the FD cross-check is sensitive and the pure-degeneracy interpretation remains unresolved.

## Observation (not a research decision)

**A. CLEAR MULTI-MODE STRUCTURE OBSERVED**, scoped to the finite deterministic seed domains and cutoffs tested. Multi-mode clusters occur in both early controls and the targeted failure cohort; the structure is not unique to the failure window. The 287.536 s row is a strong diagnostic wrong-but-sharp candidate in this sampled landscape, but it is not proof that this mechanism explains all Floor01 failures. Pure local degeneracy is not confirmed; the exact optimizer Hessian has full rank in the sampled representatives, while the FD cross-check is sensitive. No classifier, runtime threshold, fusion logic, or next-stage recommendation was produced.

## Limitations

- Finite deterministic seeds cannot enumerate all attraction basins; wide seeds were used only on the predeclared targeted cohort.
- Mode counts depend on finite translation/rotation cutoffs and the diagnostic stable-mode tag.
- Basin fractions and entropy are empirical over the sampled converged stable-seed domain, not posterior probabilities.
- PCL's analytic Hessian is the optimizer-reported local score Hessian, not Fisher information or covariance. Target-cell neighborhoods are recomputed by radius search; large finite-difference disagreement/step sensitivity limits structural curvature claims.
- GT is post-hoc only. “Nearest stable mode” is undefined for the 9 frames with `K=0`; cluster identity mismatch alone does not mean larger pose deviation.
- All outputs are offline diagnostics and do not establish runtime reliability or real-time compute cost.
